# Altara — public research

**Researched:** 22 November 2026
**Purpose:** business context for this independent proof-of-concept.
**Important:** ClientFlow AI is a personal portfolio project. It is **not** commissioned by, connected to,
or endorsed by Altara. Everything below is from public sources — their websites, their public job
adverts and public regulatory material. No private, client or employee information is used anywhere in
this project, and no data in the demo comes from the firm.

---

## A. Altara Group

Altara Group publicly describes itself as bringing together **marketing, recruitment and legal
expertise under one roof**, positioning itself as a professional services group working with businesses
across the GCC. Its LinkedIn tagline is "Strategy. People. Protection."

The advertisement for the role this project is inspired by describes the group as:

> "a fast-growing Dubai-based group of professional services and e-commerce businesses spanning law,
> HR and recruitment, digital marketing and consumer brands… building a technology-enabled operating
> model, using AI and automation to improve client service, accelerate growth, improve visibility and
> reduce repetitive administrative work."

That is four operating businesses sharing one back office:

| Arm | What it does publicly |
|---|---|
| Legal | Altara Law — full-service Dubai firm |
| HR & recruitment | PRIME HR ADVISORY |
| Digital marketing | Altara Social |
| E-commerce | Consumer brands, Shopify stores (UAE and international) |

**Sources**

## E. The advertised role (design brief)

From the public advert — parameters this project is designed against:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Titles | AI Automation Specialist / AI & Business Systems Engineer |
| Mandate | "deliver at least five meaningful production automations or AI-enabled workflows, with measurable business impact, clear documentation and appropriate security controls" within 90 days |
| Explicitly not | "a purely strategic or advisory role" |
| Workstreams | Growth automation (lead capture, instant response, follow-up, CRM, ad-performance monitoring, stalled deals, e-commerce order/inventory) · Operations (inbox triage, reminders, document preparation, management reporting, matter updates) · Integrations via APIs and webhooks |
| Security requirements | least privilege, access controls, audit logging, human approval on client-facing/legal/employment/financial decisions, no confidential data in consumer AI tools, secure credential management |
| Governance | "central register of systems and agents" including purpose, owner, integrations, permissions, data access, status |
| Success measures | hours of repetitive work eliminated · reduced missed follow-ups · faster response times · reporting accuracy · adoption · attributable revenue · reliability · security compliance |
| Nice-to-haves | M365/Graph, Pipedrive or similar CRM, legal practice-management systems, SharePoint, Shopify/WooCommerce, Xero, Meta ads, AI APIs and agent frameworks, RAG, UAE PDPL/DIFC/ADGM data protection |
| Package | AED 10,000–12,000/month, UAE visa, medical, flight allowance, performance bonus |
| Location | Dubai, office-based, five days per week |

## F. Market and regulatory context

| Fact | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| UAE PDPL | Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, in force 2 Jan 2022 — consent as default basis, records of processing, breach notification to the UAE Data Office | u.ae data protection pages |
| DIFC AI guidance | DIFC Courts Practical Guidance Note No. 2 of 2023 — parties declare AI-generated content; the lawyer remains responsible | DIFC Courts |
| Adoption vs governance | 2026 surveys put generative-AI use among legal professionals around 69%, while ~43% of firms had no AI policy | UAE press coverage of Thomson Reuters / Wolters Kluwer surveys |
| E-invoicing | Voluntary pilot Jul 2026; mandatory 1 Jan 2027 for AED 50m+ revenue, 1 Jul 2027 for others; Peppol 5-corner model | UAE Cabinet Decision 106/2025 and ministerial decisions |
| Market size | UAE legal services ≈ US$5bn (2024) → US$7.6bn by 2030 | Grand View Research |
